Dragon Age:The Veil

The Dragon Age series, developed by BioWare, is one of the most revered fantasy RPG franchises. Set in the Thedas world, it features a rich tapestry of lore, politics, magic, and conflict. Central to the lore of Dragon Age is the concept of the Veil, a mysterious boundary separating the material world from the Fade—the realm of dreams and spirits.
